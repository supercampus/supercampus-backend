## Scope ladder

Scope is what separates these personas; not one of them is a hard-coded role.

| role | scope | reach |
|---|---|---|
| `student` | `own` | Sees their own academic record, orders, passes and fees |
| `staff` | `assigned` | Teaches assigned sections and records their attendance and marks |
| `class_advisor` | `assigned` | Faculty grants plus approval over the section they advise |
| `hod` | `department` | Runs one department: its programmes, subjects, timetable and results |
| `principal` | `institution` | Institution-wide academic authority, including timetable publication |
| `librarian` | `institution` | Runs the library: catalogue, visits and passes |
| `security` | `institution` | Works the gate: verifies passes, logs visitors and scans |
| `warden` | `institution` | Runs one hostel and approves the outpasses of its residents |
| `accountant` | `institution` | Fees, refunds, vendor payments and exam eligibility on the finance side |
| `manager` | `institution` | Runs campus operations: vendors, transport, hostel and canteen |
| `owner` | `institution` | Owns one campus shop and controls its menu, orders and till |
| `captain` | `institution` | Works a campus shop counter: takes and fulfils orders |
| `superadmin` | `all` | Unrestricted access across every module |

`assigned` is the wire value the app reads as `PermissionScope.section`.
